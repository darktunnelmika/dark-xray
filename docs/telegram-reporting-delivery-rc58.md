# Telegram reporting and subscription delivery — 0.10.0-rc58

Daily forum reports previously summed every created order, including unpaid orders, and the owner bot included other shops. Operations and growth reports used order `updated_at`, so provisioning or first connection could move a historical sale into another reporting day. Reports now scope to the bot's shop and use the first matching successful payment, deduplicated by order. Representative marketplace sales use the successful wallet debit and exclude refunded orders. Currency totals remain separate. Wallet topups are shown separately from sales, and dashboard monetary fields remain IRT only. Existing order amounts, prices, credits and ledger data are not rewritten.

Forum events now explain the action in Persian with account/service/order information, monetary units and an explicit date/time zone. Raw synchronization messages and security-event details are not emitted. Each completed audit delivery advances its cursor, preventing successful messages from being replayed when a later send fails. Activation events use the existing activation message rather than a second raw audit message.

New purchases and “receive connection” use the same service card: product, purchased amount, quota/remaining volume, duration, IP policy, activation/expiry, connection link, copy button, portal and usage controls. Locally generated PNG QR images encode the exact configured subscription/main config/portal payload and honor `show_qr`, delivery mode and portal flags. A photo upload failure still delivers the full link. Unlimited-IP plans display unlimited users. Dates use the panel time zone explicitly. Existing message history is preserved.

Wallet preset labels now include Persian amounts and تومان. Representative creation collects its existing four fields step by step, supports a generated password, and creates the account only after final confirmation. Volume and unlimited credits retain their original separate accounting rules.

Read-only Telegram API calls retry one stale-connection failure. Outgoing mutations are not automatically retried. Brief transport failures reconnect without repeated raw error reports; an outage lasting a minute produces a rate-limited Persian notice and a recovery notice when connectivity returns.

Validation: regression coverage includes unpaid orders, owner isolation, multiple currencies, duplicate payment attempts, activation on a different day, original confirmation timestamps, Tehran day boundaries, representative refunds, original ledger preservation, access checks, delivery modes, QR flags and upload failure, multipart photo delivery, retry behavior, partial forum delivery and guided representative creation. Additional independent QR decoding verified subscription/config/both/portal PNGs and their JPEG-compressed variants against the exact original payload.
