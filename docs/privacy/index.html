DarkXray Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

DarkXray is a VPN client. It uses Android VpnService because VPN connectivity is the core functionality of the app.

Data handled by DarkXray

The app handles VPN/proxy configuration links and subscription URLs supplied by the user, network traffic required to provide the VPN connection, and local connection diagnostics such as latency, connection state, session duration, and app-level upload/download counters.

Collection and sharing

The current DarkXray mobile client does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, account sign-in, or telemetry sent to a DarkXray analytics service. Configuration data and app settings are stored locally on the device. Subscription requests are sent directly to the subscription URL provided by the user. VPN traffic is sent through the VPN or proxy endpoint selected by the user.

VpnService

DarkXray uses Android VpnService only to create the user-requested device-level VPN tunnel. It does not use VpnService to inject advertisements, redirect advertising traffic, or monetize browsing traffic. Google Play builds reject connection profiles that do not provide an encrypted path to the VPN/proxy endpoint.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored profiles, subscription URLs, settings, and diagnostics remain on the device until the user removes them, chooses Reset Local Data, clears app storage, or uninstalls DarkXray.

Security

DarkXray uses Android app-private storage, Android VpnService, and the XTLS Xray core. Cleartext HTTP subscription URLs are not permitted in the Google Play build.

Third-party endpoints

VPN servers and subscription providers are independently operated endpoints. Their logging, retention, and privacy practices are controlled by their operators. Users should review the privacy policy of the provider whose endpoint they choose.

Children

DarkXray is a general-purpose network utility and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when DarkXray changes how it handles data. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
